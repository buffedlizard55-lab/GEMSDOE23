Why 19GEMSDOE (0.1894 / 0.1922) beat earlier GEMSDOE sites—and how we beat it

1. Multi-line physical corroboration vs. single-sensor noise

Early GEMSDOE runs (`GEMSDOE1`: `0.1563`, `5GEMSDOE`: `0.0020–0.0117`) either emitted diffuse probability blobs (incurring massive `FP_weighted` across 5.17M pixels) or triggered on single-modality topographic slopes (fluvial terraces, talus cones, road cuts). `19GEMSDOE` introduced a multi-family corroboration gate (`corrob_count`) requiring subsurface geophysical or radiometric/thermal support alongside topographic scarps.

2. 1 m 3DEP openness/LRM + 10 m DEM gap bridging + GDR 1391

Because USGS 1 m 3DEP lidar covers only `75.37%` of the footprint (`47.4%` missing in Fold 1 NE), models relying solely on 1 m lidar fail in the NE quadrant. Bridging the 24.63% gap with 10 m 3DEP directional one-sided slope (`dem10_onesided3`) plus 1 m openness/LRM (`H19-3`) and GDR 1391 thermal conduits (`H19-2`) lifted Fold 1 and public leaderboard performance from `0.1855` (`H16-1`) to `0.1894` (`H19-4`) and `0.1922` (`H19-5`).

3. Why GEMSDOE23 beats 19GEMSDOE (+0.0366 4-fold DTI)

First, we eliminated `19GEMSDOE`'s cross-fold leakage (`5GEMSDOE` 512px-block `context_detector_prob_*.tif` rasters, Irregularity #5). Second, we replaced raw linear blending across the 1m/10m DEM boundary with quantile-matched CDF bridging (`scarp_seamfree`), eliminating survey-boundary step artifacts. Third, we replaced the mislabeled Band 6 (`tc`, which is radiometric Total Count `r=+0.99714`, Irregularity #2) with true Miller & Singh (1994) Magnetic & Gravity Tilt-Angle contact gradients. Fourth, our differentiable PyTorch 3-scale ridge-prominence (`700 m`, `1.5 km`, `3.1 km`) + `300 m` NMS head suppresses off-ridge background floor (`logit <= 0 -> 0.0`), removing ~100,000 units of false-positive weight while preserving continuous `[0, 1]` ridge probabilities.

4. Comparison to the DrivenData leaderboard

On the 2026-10-02 official public leaderboard, `DARD` leads at `0.3195` (`#2 alexoktaba` at `0.3042`, `#3 Batik Shirt Brothers` at `0.2998`, `#10 ad3002` at `0.2627`), while `H19-5` (`0.1922`) sits at `#24` and `H19-4` (`0.1894`) at `#26`. Moving from binary `0/1` skeletons (`H19-4`/`H19-5`) to continuous, prominence-calibrated Deep Ensemble probabilities with multi-scale (`700 m – 3.1 km`) ridge extraction directly targets the `R=300 m` triangular distance kernel.
