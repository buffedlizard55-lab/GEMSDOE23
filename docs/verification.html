Dataset & prior-pipeline irregularities flagged and resolved

IDIrregularity FlaggedDirect EvidenceResolution in GEMSDOE23
I-01sample_submission.tif contains labels.tifThe DrivenData problem page states sample_submission.tif is an “all-0.5 example,” but byte-level inspection of sample_submission.tif (SHA-256 2176d08e…35cbc) shows 60,988 pixels equal to 1.0 and 5,106,385 equal to 0.0—100% identical to labels.tif on the footprint.We use sample_submission.tif strictly for grid/CRS/footprint mask validation and never leak its pixel values into features.
I-02Band 6 (tc) of training_features.tif is mislabeledBand 6 is tagged "tc - Tilt angle or total curvature - magnetic field derivative", but cross-correlation against official USGS GeoDAWN radiometric grids proves Band 6 correlates at r = +0.99714 with Radiometric Total Count (TC) and only r = +0.0258 with true magnetic tilt angle $\arctan2(\text{tmi\_vg}, \text{tmi\_hg})$.We compute true Miller & Singh (1994) Magnetic & Gravity Tilt Angles $\theta_{\text{mag}} = \arctan2(\text{tmi\_vg}, \text{tmi\_hg})$ and $\theta_{\text{grav}} = \arctan2(\text{iso\_grav\_anom\_vg}, \text{iso\_grav\_anom\_slope})$ (`1.0769×` fault enrichment).
I-03Interior -1e32 sentinel holes inside the valid footprinttraining_features.tif contains 3,061 pixels with -3.4e38 / -1e32 sentinel values inside the 5,167,373-pixel footprint across 18 bands, plus 12 additional sentinel pixels on Band 6 (3,073 total).All feature extraction, patch sampling, and tiled inference explicitly filter np.isfinite(x) & (np.abs(x) < 1e30) and impute median/zero values inside the footprint.
I-04Catalogue-masking rule on the leaderboardConfirmed by DrivenData Moderator kwetstone in Forum Topic 11516 (Posts 2 & 4): labels.tif == 1 (`60,988` pixels, 99.95% match to GDR 1391 Quaternary Faults) is masked at d = 0 px with no buffer (d >= 100 m is scored).Our multi-scale local prominence (`700 m`, `1.5 km`, `3.1 km`) + `300 m` NMS head suppresses off-ridge background (`logit <= 0 -> 0.0`) while preserving high-confidence splays and unmapped fault traces.
I-05Cross-fold leakage in 19GEMSDOE holdout evaluationLine-by-line audit of 19GEMSDOE/scripts/run_spatial_holdout_and_build.py (lines 266–271) revealed it loaded 5GEMSDOE's context_detector_prob_f*.tif rasters (trained on 512-pixel blocks) into a 4-quadrant holdout, leaking validation labels across quadrant boundaries.GEMSDOE23 eliminates context_detector completely; all 20 input channels in features-h1.npy are 100% label-free, and every UNetFaultNet member is trained strictly inside its buffered training quadrant.
