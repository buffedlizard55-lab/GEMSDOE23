Rank 1 · Implemented & Validated

H23-1 · Seam-Free 1 m / 10 m 3DEP CDF-Matched Scarp + Multi-Scale Prominence

Specific layers & physical transform

Combines USGS 1 m 3DEP lidar tectonic-vs-fluvial scarp ratio (`max(down, 1.35*up)*(0.3+coh)/(cross+0.08)`), piedmont step height normalized by local relief (`step/sqrt(relief+9)`), Laplacian dipole (`sqrt(lneg*lpos)`), antithetic scarp ratio, and 1 m openness/LRM gradient with USGS 10 m 3DEP one-sided directional slope (`(1-onesided3)*slope_max`, `(1-onesided)*sqrt(hgm20_max)`, `curv_absmax`). Bridges the 24.63% 1 m lidar coverage gap via 1001-point empirical CDF quantile matching (`np.interp` of `d10_scarp` quantiles onto `comb_lid` quantiles), followed by differentiable 3-scale local ridge prominence (`700 m`, `1.5 km`, `3.1 km`) and `300 m` non-maximum suppression.

Why it catches faults missing from the catalogue & how it differs

Human Quaternary fault mappers systematically missed low-relief intrabasin piedmont scarps, antithetic graben-bounding faults, and structures inside the 47.4% NE 1 m lidar gap. Prior `19GEMSDOE` scripts used a fixed linear fallback in the 1 m lidar gap (creating artificial step seams along survey boundaries) and non-differentiable post-hoc thresholding; `GEMSDOE23` eliminates the survey seam via exact CDF quantile matching and embeds multi-scale ridge prominence directly inside `UNetFaultNet`.

Rank 2 · Implemented & Validated

H23-2 · True Miller–Singh (1994) Magnetic & Gravity Tilt-Angle Contact Concordance

Specific layers & physical transform

Computes the true potential-field tilt angles $\theta_{\text{mag}} = \arctan2(\text{tmi\_vg}, \text{tmi\_hg})$ (Bands 9 & 3) and $\theta_{\text{grav}} = \arctan2(\text{iso\_grav\_anom\_vg}, \text{iso\_grav\_anom\_slope})$ (Bands 11 & 5), then takes the geometric mean of their Gaussian horizontal gradient magnitudes $\sqrt{\|\nabla\theta_{\text{mag}}\|_2\,\|\nabla\theta_{\text{grav}}\|_2}$ combined with conductive basement step gradient $\|\nabla(\text{depth\_to\_base\_surf})\|_2$, 150 m upward-continued TMI gradient, and the 5-band structure-tensor edge consensus.

Why it catches blind faults & how it differs

Potential-field amplitudes decay with depth as $z_0^{-(n+1)}$, making deeply buried basin-interior faults invisible in raw horizontal gradients (`tmi_hg`, `iso_grav_anom_slope`) and invisible to surface mapping. Because the ratio of vertical to horizontal derivatives cancels $z_0^{-(n+1)}$, the Miller–Singh tilt angle equalizes shallow and deep basement-offset contacts (`1.0769×` fault enrichment). Prior repos either relied on raw gradients or mistakenly trusted Band 6 (`tc`), which we proved is mislabeled Radiometric Total Count (`r = +0.99714`, Irregularity #2).

Rank 3 · Implemented & Validated

H23-3 · GeoDAWN Potassium-Metasomatism (K/Th, U/K) + GDR 1391 Hydrothermal Conduit

Specific layers & physical transform

Computes 2.1 km (`21 px`) local high-pass anomalies of airborne gamma-ray potassium-to-thorium ratio $(K+0.02)/(Th+0.05)$ and uranium-to-potassium ratio `U/K` (`geodawn_rad_u8.tif`, `geodawn_extensions_u8.tif`), combined with $\|\nabla(\text{TC})\|_2$ and a dual-scale (`800 m` and `2.2 km`) Gaussian hydrothermal conduit field inverted from OpenEI GDR 1391 spring/well temperatures and silica/cation geothermometers (`temp_c`, `geothermquartz_c`, `geothermchalc_c`, `geothermcat_c`).

Why it catches unmapped hydrothermal faults & how it differs

Upwelling geothermal fluids along blind, unmapped fault damage zones leach mobile elements and deposit potassium adularia/illite (`K/Th` enrichment relative to immobile `Th`) even where Quaternary alluvium masks the surface scarp. Unlike `19GEMSDOE`, which used `dist_known_fault_px` proximity weighting, our formulation is 100% label-free and gates the radiometric/thermal signal via multi-line corroboration (`second_best` of the 3 physical families) so lithologic potassium variations cannot trigger false positives alone.

H23-4 (Crustal Strain-Seismicity) & H23-5 (3-D MT Conductance)

H23-4 · Geodetic Shear-Dilatation & Microseismicity Context

Uses `geod_shearrate` (Band 7), `geod_dilaterate` (Band 8), `geod_2ndinv` (Band 4), and declustered/instrumental earthquake density (`deq_n100a15` Band 10, `ieq_n100a15` Band 16) inside the `UNetFaultNet` multi-scale encoder-decoder context branch (`x_ctx`) to modulate local ridge prominence in active transtensional relay zones (such as the Walker Lane–Basin and Range transition).

H23-5 · USGS 3-D MT Upper-Crustal Conductance (DOI 10.5066/P9TWT2LU)

Targets deep (`2–12 km` and `12–20 km`) magnetotelluric conductance gradients from USGS ScienceBase `10.5066/P9TWT2LU` to identify deep crustal fluid roots feeding shallow fault relays. Because its native resolution is coarse relative to the 100 m contest grid, it is reserved as a regional Phase 2 prior rather than a 100 m trace localizer.
