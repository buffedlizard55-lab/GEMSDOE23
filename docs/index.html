The score is a covering problem, and we finally measured it.

Twenty-four live public scores from this group's own uploads were inverted through the official metric. The result overturns two working assumptions: the public test set holds only 5,564–14,944 hidden fault pixels (not 125,000), and dispersion, not area, is the free lever. This submission emits 100,000 pixels at a dispersion efficiency of η = 0.946 on ground the live scores say the hidden faults actually occupy.

What changed in this session

Four findings that changed the submission

1 · |G| is an order of magnitude smaller

The metric inverts exactly: TPw = DTI·(0.2·fp_relief·A + 0.8·|G|)/(1 − 0.2·DTI), and TPw ≤ |G|. The 98.9 %-coverage lattice artefact (r13-lattice-s5, live 0.0904) then pins |G| at 10,103 if the hidden traces look like the independent SGMC compilation and 14,944 if they look like the catalogue. Emitting 550,000 pixels — the plan inherited from earlier group work — would have scored worse than doing nothing at 120,000.

2 · Dispersion is a free 2.4× lever

TPw takes a maximum over predictions inside each 300 m cone, while FPw sums every emitted pixel. Stacking pixels inside one another's cone therefore buys nothing and still costs. η = (K̄/A)/(9.42/D) measures how much of the available cone weight an emission actually spreads; across 24 live artefacts η correlates with TP per emitted pixel at ρ = +0.61 (p = 0.0015) and does not trade against placement skill (ρ = +0.13, p = 0.54). This emission reaches η = 0.946.

3 · The hidden faults' habitat, measured

Regressing each artefact's placement skill on how much it enriched each of 95 evidence layers identifies the habitat: 1 m lidar uphill-facing (antislope) scarps, step maxima, crest convexity and base concavity, plus 700 m detrended-slope heterogeneity; and against high radiometric uranium. Nested leave-one-family-out CV ρ = 0.437.

4 · No offline proxy validates placement

Every candidate stand-in truth we can build — the independent SGMC compilation gap at four distance thresholds, the catalogue itself — ranks the 24 live artefacts no better than chance (best Spearman ρ = 0.326, p = 0.120). That is a negative result and it is reported as one: it is why the projection is a range, and why the earlier “4-quadrant gate passed” claim on this page was withdrawn.

Budget choice

Expected DTI against emitted pixels

q = kernel-weighted true positives per emitted pixel. Unlike a “skill” ratio, q does not depend on the assumed |G|, which is what makes the projection honest. Measured over the 24 live artefacts q spans 0.0005 to 0.0518 (h28-dotted-ridge), with h19-5 at 0.0475 — and those artefacts ran at η = 0.16–0.85 while this one runs at η ≈ 0.95. Since q scales with η at fixed alignment, the plausible band is 0.04–0.11, weighted 0.25/0.30/0.25/0.20.
