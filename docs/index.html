Six findings from fitting the fault statistic before touching the model

These findings were first computed in an earlier hash-verified input restore; the three core competition rasters and all external layers are present again in this checkout (restore receipt re-verified), so the fitting script can be re-run at any time to reproduce them.

1 · The catalogue is a clustered, self-similar fault population

3,199 traces; length exponent a = 3.15 above 3 km; nearest-larger-neighbour x = 1.54 against the Bour & Davy prediction 1.20–1.55; correlation dimension ≈ 1.5–1.7; the normalised correlation sum is 3.6–4.2× random between 0.5 and 2 km and still 1.15× at 30 km. Details and figures →

2 · H24 failed its own audit

A spectral line at exactly the 400 m Area 2 traverse spacing (strength 243 vs a control 95th percentile of 10, rank p = 0.016), 931 dots closer than the stated 400 m (equal-score plateaus accepted together), and an arrangement that is statistically a lattice. Removed in H29/H30.

3 · The geometric prior is real but small for the faults that matter

Faults missing from the catalogue (independent SGMC sample) are enriched ×1.8 at 200–400 m and ×2.1 in the continuation wedge beyond tips, but ≈ 1 beyond 1 km; the continuation zone covers 0.7 % of the domain. Live scores reward no concentration near known faults. The prior is therefore wired in as a tie-break (≤ 0.003 rank units, ~1 % of dots move).

4 · Dispersal is not the free lever it was presented as

In the one controlled pair (same score, same 155,021 pixels) dispersal raised K̄ 2.7× and lowered skill 2.6×, leaving TP — and DTI (0.1152 → 0.1193) — unchanged. The “0.12–0.34 projected” headline assumed skill would transfer; no score forecast is supported.

5 · Arrangement orders the live scores

Signed divergence of an emission's pair statistics from the catalogue's: Spearman -0.66 with live DTI (n = 23, post-hoc bins, correlational). Over-clustered detectors average 0.034, lattices 0.093, mildly-less-clustered emissions 0.170. H24 was in the lattice bin; H30 is in the best one.

6 · Reproducibility, prior-run record

H24 rebuilds bit-for-bit from public inputs; all 23 live-scored artefacts re-fetch with exact pixel-count identity; the full habitat refit reproduces the committed model only when anchors are listed in the committed order (tie-breaking on a duplicate anchor, flag I-20).

Earlier findings that still stand: |G| is 5,564–14,944 pixels, not 125,000; the habitat is lidar uphill-facing scarps, steps and crests plus 700 m detrended-slope heterogeneity and low radiometric U (nested-CV ρ = 0.437); no offline proxy truth ranks the live artefacts better than chance (best ρ = 0.326, p = 0.120).
