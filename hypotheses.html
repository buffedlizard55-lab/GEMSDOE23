New hypotheses

Current session (2026-10-02, data restored and verified)

H-38 … H-42: five new mechanisms, ranked; H-38/H-39 implemented, blocked-validated, and nested-CV tested

Five genuinely new geological hypotheses were designed against the restored, hash-verified rasters. H-38 and H-39 are implemented (src/gems/orientation.py, unit-tested) and were validated this session on the spatially blocked SGMC-proxy holdout (same folds, target and budget as the deep-ensemble admission gate), against the 24 live scores, and in the 15-fold leave-one-family-out habitat nested-CV regression. The honest verdict: both are rejected as standalone emissions and neither survives unforced nested habitat CV — neither beats a random emission on any blocked fold (0/5), and in 15-fold nested leave-one-family-out habitat CV H-39 ranks 37–50/96 by univariate |Spearman| (well below the top-8 cutoff 0.5548), is selected in 0/15 folds (leaving nested-CV Spearman unchanged at 0.437391), and degrades CV when the raw layer is force-appended (0.383478, Δ = −0.053913 at |G|=6,000). Both H-38 and H-39 are therefore closed and neither justifies a submission slot. H-40 … H-42 remain proposed with their free official sources named and, for H-40, obtainability verified. No numeric ΔDTI is claimed anywhere on this page.

1H-38Cross-family azimuth (orientation) coherence. A fault is a linear structure: require that independent lineament families are locally anisotropic and strike the same way, scoring Aij = cohi·cohj·cos²(θi−θj).Structure tensors (σ = 300 m) of the official det_elev, rtp, iso_grav_anom, cond_surf, depth_to_base_surf bands ⊕ the lidar product's own structure-tensor strike/coh100 channels.Magnitude-only corroboration (H19-4's multi-line gate, this repo's edge consensus) cannot distinguish two fields that are both strong at a place but strike differently — a coincidence, not a fault. Catalogue faults were mapped largely from magnitude evidence, so orientation agreement is an independent discriminator for the unmapped remainder. Method precedent: structure-tensor edge analysis of potential fields (Sertcelik & Kafadar 2012, DOI 10.1016/j.jappgeo.2012.06.004); horizontal-gradient ridges (Blakely & Simpson 1986, DOI 10.1190/1.1442103).No layer, transform or sibling artefact ever compares azimuths across families; lid_strike exists in the layer bank but is never cross-checked. The kernel bug this session's unit test caught (cos²(2Δθ) scoring perpendicular lines as agreeing) shows why the transform must be tested, not assumed.Medium-high potential; ordinal only — the blocked-validation record below is the evidence, and it is proxy evidence.Medium (done)Implemented + validated this session. Blocked SGMC-proxy result: mean DTI 0.0075 at the 1.22 % budget vs random 0.0995 and the fixed h19-5 emission 0.1030 (0/5 folds beat random, 0/5 beat the fixed h19-5 emission). Live-score consistency ρ = -0.06. Stop condition: does not beat random on a majority of folds — fired (0/5).
2H-39Shallow-residual potential-field edges. Upward continuation attenuates a wavelength λ by exp(−2πh/λ); the residual (field − continuation) keeps only the shallowest sources, so its edge ridges in thick cover are young, cover-cutting structures.rtp − upward_continuation(rtp, 500 m and 2,000 m) ⊕ edge magnitude ⊕ depth_to_base_surf cover-thickness context (h39_resid_edge_500m, h39_resid_edge_2000m, h39_resid_edge_thickcover).The catalogue is dominated by mapped bedrock structure; deep-persistent magnetic edges are therefore mostly already catalogued. A young Quaternary fault cutting alluvial cover is a shallow, short-wavelength source that the subtraction isolates. USGS applied exactly this residual at Fort Irwin (Great-Basin-adjacent) to 'enhance anomalies produced by shallow or exposed magnetic sources' (USGS OFR 2013-1024); separation theory: Jacobsen 1987, DOI 10.1190/1.1442376, Blakely 1995.The layer bank has native-support gradients and one 150 m upward-continued magnitude layer (ext_TMI_up150); no residual, no residual-edge, no cover-context product exists. H-35 (above) proposes the complement (deep persistence); this targets the shallow end.Medium potential; ordinal only.Low-medium (done)Implemented + validated + nested-CV tested this session. 4H-41Direction-resolved fault-tip and step-over lobes. Step-overs host ~40 % of the INGENIOUS area's favourable structural settings and terminations ~17 % (Faulds & Hinz 2015; Stanford SGP-TR 2026 update, which maps FSS points for the INGENIOUS study area); the staff ruling says 'new fault' includes newly mapped geometry of existing systems.Trace skeletons of labels.tif ⊕ SGMC traces (both in-repo) → tip azimuths → anisotropic wing-crack lobes (30–45° off tip) and relay ramps between overlapping, like-dipping traces.Splays and relay strands nucleate at tips and step-overs of known faults — geometry the catalogue truncates. The isotropic clusterprior (enrichment ×2.3 within 1 km of long faults) cannot express direction; wing-crack lobes are where the missing short traces physically must be.The sibling H19-1 used isotropic σ = 1.8/2.5 km tip/step-over Gaussians; this repo's clusterprior is isotropic proximity. 5H-42Playa / basin-margin discharge association. Great Basin Quaternary faults bound basins and control discharge; the weights-of-evidence geothermal GIS work found favourability in shallow-groundwater/basin-floor settings and a preference for NE-trending structures within 5 km (Coolbaugh et al. 2002, GRC Transactions; NBMG Map 151).det_elev flatness + local relief adjacency ⊕ cond_surf ⊕ the in-repo GDR spring/well CSV → distance-to-basin-floor and basin-margin-edge density.Catalogue mapping concentrated in bedrock/uplands; basin-margin strands under alluvium near playas are systematically under-mapped — the same 'sparse historical fieldwork' premise the catalogue-gap hypothesis rests on.The spring layers are pure distance/density; no playa, basin-floor or margin-geometry term exists in the 95-layer bank or any sibling artefact.Low-medium potential (targets geothermal systems more than fault traces); ordinal only.Low-mediumProposed. What the blocked validation can and cannot say. The SGMC proxy is the only independent uncatalogued-fault truth available offline, and the offline-proxy audit measured that no available proxy ranks the 24 live artefacts above chance — so a proxy win is necessary but not sufficient for a slot, and the live-score consistency check (enrichment of each new layer inside the live-scored artefacts' pixels vs their |G|-inverted skill) is reported alongside it. A submission slot is not authorised by anything on this page; the current-holdout registry remains the release gate.
